Export License Screener

Enter a part number or a plain-language description of a piece of equipment to get a quick, first-pass read on whether it is likely to need a US export license — which Commerce Control List category and ECCN it tends to fall under, and why. This is a triage aid, not a classification.

Not legal or compliance advice. This tool does a keyword screen against a small, illustrative knowledge base of common dual-use categories. It cannot and does not produce an official classification. The real export-control outcome depends on the precise technical parameters of the item, its jurisdiction (EAR vs. ITAR vs. NRC/DOE), the destination, the end-user and the end-use — and on the current regulations, which change frequently. Always confirm with the EAR Commerce Control List, a self-classification or CCATS, and a licensed export-compliance professional before shipping. Edit export-license.json to expand the rules.
