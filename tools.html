tools

Small utilities and experiments.

Export License Screener → Enter a part number or description of a piece of equipment to get a quick first-pass read on whether it likely needs a US export license — the probable CCL category, ECCN and control reasons, plus destination flags. Moat Deleter → Identify the structural moat a sector or business relies on, classify the power behind it, expose its load-bearing weakness, and follow a step-by-step playbook to drain it. NIS2 Requirement Tracker → Look up EU NIS2 supply-chain and cybersecurity requirements and track compliance. What I Pay For a… → A running daily budget of the small recurring things — a cup of coffee, a smoothie — with the cost per day, a short note on each, and live monthly and yearly totals so you can see your real overhead and where to trim.
