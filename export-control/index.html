Export Control Library

A memo to file on U.S. export controls, one each morning. Part One records the developments of the preceding day with labels, dates and links to the source. Part Two teaches one point of export control law in depth, following a fixed curriculum across the ITAR, the EAR, sanctions, enforcement, allied regimes, and the places where export controls meet cybersecurity and government contracts. November 27, 2026 · B · USML categories USML Category VIII, Aircraft and Related Articles → Category VIII enumerates thirteen populated classes of aircraft in (a), shipboard launch and recovery equipment in (d), DoD funded developmental aircraft in (f), twenty-two populated classes of parts and systems in (h), and related technical data and services in (i), with SME on (a)(1)-(5), (a)(7), (a)(8), (a)(15), and (h)(20) and MT at 300 km. Every other specially designed military aircraft and part is 9A610. Since Sept. 15, 2025, (h)(1) keeps listed-aircraft parts on the USML when reused in foreign advanced military aircraft (defined in 121.0 with the 2023 production year), which replaced the KF-21 temporary note. Since Oct. 13, 2026, (a)(8) excludes survivability-enhanced aircraft, and 120.54(a)(8)-(9) free the movement of secured ASE aboard them. The Oct. 1, 2026 proposed rule would reserve (h)(10) radar altimeters. Brokering any Category VIII aircraft or UAV needs approval under 129.4(a)(2)(v).
