Export Control Library

A memo to file on U.S. export controls, one each morning. Part One records the developments of the preceding day with labels, dates and links to the source. Part Two teaches one point of export control law in depth, following a fixed curriculum across the ITAR, the EAR, sanctions, enforcement, allied regimes, and the places where export controls meet cybersecurity and government contracts. This index lists every brief with the rule it teaches and the instruments it cites.
